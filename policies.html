Privacy Policy

ApnaScore ("we", "our") respects your privacy. Guest Mode stores matches, teams, and scorecards locally on your device. Registered accounts store profile and tournament records securely in our database. Payment transactions are processed securely via Razorpay; we never store your card, CVV, or UPI PIN details.

Terms & Conditions

ApnaScore provides digital cricket scoring and tournament management tools. Free tier includes unlimited Simple Matches and 1 tournament (up to 4 teams). Tournament Manager Pro unlocks advanced tournament features per tournament or monthly as selected at checkout. Organizers are responsible for team and match accuracy.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

If a payment fails and an amount is debited from your bank/UPI account, it is automatically refunded by Razorpay within 5–7 business days. Duplicate accidental charges for the same tournament are eligible for a full refund within 7 days of purchase by contacting support@apnascore.in with your Receipt Number.
