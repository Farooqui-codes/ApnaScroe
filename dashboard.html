About ApnaScore • Features, Offline, FAQs & Developer ▾

What is ApnaScore?

ApnaScore is a modern, offline-first cricket scoring platform built especially for local, gully, street, and village cricket tournaments. It runs 100% offline in remote grounds without internet, stores balls securely on your device, and syncs automatically whenever you reconnect.

Key Features

  • 100% Offline Scoring: Score entire matches even with zero internet connectivity.
  • Custom Local Rules: Last-man batting, one-tip-one-hand, unlimited wickets, custom extras.
  • Live Scorecard Sharing: Share instant live scorecards and WhatsApp match summaries.
  • Tournaments & NRR: Create tournaments, group stages, auto-calculated Net Run Rate, and points tables.
  • Detailed Charts & PDF: Wagon wheels, worm charts, and professional PDF match reports.

How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

1. Setup Match

Enter team names, overs per innings, and your custom match rules.

2. Tap to Score

Use the sunlight-readable keypad to record runs, extras, and wickets with 1 tap.

3. Share Scorecard

Share live score links, WhatsApp score updates, and download PDF scorecards.

Offline Mode & Data Safety

Every ball is saved to IndexedDB inside your browser immediately. If your phone loses battery or network drops, your match progress is preserved. As soon as you are back online, all pending events sync to the cloud.

Pro & Pricing Summary

Scoring local matches is 100% free forever. Tournament organizers who need unlimited simultaneous tournaments, custom team logos, branded PDF certificates, and advanced tournament leaderboards can upgrade to ApnaScore Pro.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I score without creating an account?

Yes! Guest Mode lets you score matches immediately. You can upgrade to a permanent account anytime without losing your local matches.

Can I undo a wrong ball entry?

Yes, tap the "Undo" button on the scoring screen or tap any ball in the recent balls strip to edit or delete it.
